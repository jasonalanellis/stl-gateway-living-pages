Neighborhood Guide

Soulard

Soulard is St. Louis's oldest neighborhood — the 1779 Soulard Farmers Market is the country's longest-running, the cobblestone streets still feel 19th-century, and the brick row houses are some of the most beautiful in the Midwest.
