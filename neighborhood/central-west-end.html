Central West End

The Central West End is St. Louis's most walkable upscale neighborhood — the Cathedral Basilica's mosaics are world-class, the restaurants along Euclid run the spectrum from cocktail bars to long-running French institutions, and the residential streets show off the city's Gilded Age architecture.
