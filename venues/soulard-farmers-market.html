Soulard Farmers Market

Soulard, St. Louis

Address: 730 Carroll St, St. Louis, MO 63104

Visit website →

Wikipedia entry

“The Soulard Farmers Market has been running continuously since 1779 — making it one of the oldest public markets in the country west of the Mississippi. On Saturday mornings, it's the pulse of the neighborhood: local produce, spices, fresh-baked bread, meat vendors who've been there for generations, and enough people-watching to keep you entertained for hours.”
