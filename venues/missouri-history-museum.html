“Within a half-mile of each other in Forest Park, all facing Art Hill and the central meadow, sit the Saint Louis Art Museum, the Missouri History Museum, and the Saint Louis Science Center. The Saint Louis Art Museum in its Cass Gilbert hilltop building houses one of the finest collections in the country — particular strengths in German Expressionism and pre-Columbian art — and the main collection is free.
