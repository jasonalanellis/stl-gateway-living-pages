Founder & Editor-in-Chief, STL Gateway Living

Jason Ellis edits STL Gateway Living, a local guide to the people, places, and weekend rhythms of St. Louis. The guide is built by writers and operators who live in the city.

The guide publishes weekly editorial coverage of St. Louis — neighborhoods, restaurants, weekend events, and the local-life details that residents actually use. Jason Ellis writes and edits the work, and partners with local contributors on specialized coverage.

Editorial independence: Rankings and editorial recommendations are based on independent research and reporting. Where brand links appear on service pages, they are clearly labeled (rel="sponsored") or noted as followed editorial mentions; no business relationship influences the editorial judgment of this guide.
