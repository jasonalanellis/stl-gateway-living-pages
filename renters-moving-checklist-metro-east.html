The Metro East & St. Louis Renter's Moving Checklist (2026)

Moving across the river — or just across town — comes with a paperwork trail most renters don't expect. Here's the order locals actually follow, from lease signature to final walkthrough.

Whether you're relocating from the city to the Metro East side or shifting neighborhoods within St. Louis County, a smooth move comes down to sequencing: lock in logistics early, handle utilities and address changes mid-move, and save the deep clean for last. This checklist walks through it in order.

Step 1: Book Movers and Storage Before You Pack

Reputable local movers and short-term storage book up fast around month-end, which is when most St. Louis leases turn over. Line these up first so the calendar drives everything else:

  • Two Men and a Truck (St. Louis) — full-service local moving with hourly and flat-rate crews for apartment and house moves.
  • Public Storage — short-term self-storage units across the St. Louis metro for the overlap window between move-out and move-in dates.
  • PODS — portable storage/moving containers, useful when your old and new lease dates don't line up cleanly.

Step 2: Update Your Address and Utilities Early

Do this the same week movers are booked, not the week of the move — utility transfers can take several business days to schedule.

  • File a change of address with the USPS official change-of-address service — the single most-missed step, and the one that causes lost mail for weeks after a move.
  • If you're crossing into Illinois, check registration and licensing requirements with the Illinois Secretary of State before your old plates or license expire.
  • Staying in Missouri, moving within the metro? Confirm voter and vehicle registration address updates with the State of Missouri before your next renewal notice.

Step 3: Confirm Lease Walkthrough Terms

Most St. Louis-area leases require documented condition at move-in and move-out. Photograph everything on day one, and re-read your lease's cleaning clause before move-out day — many require the unit returned "professionally cleaned," not just tidy.

Step 4: The Move-Out Clean

This is the step that determines whether a security deposit comes back in full. An empty unit shows every scuff and baseboard the furniture used to hide, and property managers know the difference between a quick sweep and a real deep clean.

That's where Clean Town & Country's move-out cleaning service comes in — a steam-first, 275°F thermal shock deep-clean built specifically for empty-unit turnovers, so the walkthrough goes smoothly on the first pass.

Bottom line: sequence the move — book logistics first, redirect your mail and paperwork mid-move, and close with a documented deep clean. Renters who follow this order get their deposits back faster and skip the second-trip scramble.
